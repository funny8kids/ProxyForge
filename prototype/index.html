Governance · Trust boundary

Trust & Tiers

Tier is a permission decision, not metadata. AI may suggest; only a human may approve. Public proxies never reach the production gateway.

Permission matrix

Authoritative · orthogonal to authorization status
TierDefault gatewaySubscriptionProduction gatewayBasis
PRIVATE Blocked Blocked Explicit grant Owned or contracted source with clear ownership
PUBLIC Blocked Experiment only Never Open source, authorization cannot be proven
VERIFIED Allowed Allowed Allowed L1–L6 passed + authorization review + human confirmation
Database-level guardCHECK (NOT (trust_tier = 'PUBLIC_PROXY' AND explicit_gateway_grant))
Even an application-layer bug cannot grant gateway access to a public proxy.
